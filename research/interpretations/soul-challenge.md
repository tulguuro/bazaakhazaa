# Точка Г / D отдельно — Сүнсний гол сорилт, Чөлөөлөх түлхүүр (D)

Researched 2026-11-13. Notes are paraphrased themes, not quotes. Karmic tails (D_inner–D_mid–D) are covered elsewhere; this file
reads **D on its own**.

## Position and our chapters

| Our key | Formula | Russian names | Our chapters |
|---|---|---|---|
| D (`bottom`) | A + B + C | точка Г, нижний угол, "главная кармическая задача", "кармическое число", "задача души" (some books) | Сүнсний гол сорилт (Main Soul Challenge), Чөлөөлөх түлхүүр (Key to Liberation) |

- **D is never 1 or 2** with digit-sum reduction (sum of A+B+C is ≥ 4; reductions of 23–66 give ≥ 3). Checked for 1900–2030.
  MC also gives no text for 1–2. MU still writes Г texts for 1 and 2 — generic, or from a school that subtracts 22.
- **What D means — sources differ on the name, agree on the idea:**
  - vikna.tv: main karmic lesson.
  - mind.university (zones.md): in the tail the 3rd number D is "the karmic task itself" (1st = character, 2nd = events).
  - MU arcana pages: Г = "karmic point / главная проработка": the recurring scenario to break; for several energies it names
    *which relationships* carry the lesson (1 siblings; 2 mother, older women, children, nature; 3 men with women/mother;
    5 children, father, bosses; 6 partners; 20 family).
  - "Матрица Судьбы. Я 2.0" (free excerpt): Г = the soul's **main task**, why the soul came — i.e. "задача души" sits at D, not C.
  - English sites (karmictail.pages.dev, theworldmag): bottom = karmic baggage / lesson to complete.
- Reading rule shared by all: D is read mostly in its minus first (what keeps repeating), then the plus = what is gained once
  the lesson is learnt. That fits our two chapters: "Challenge" = minus/repeat, "Key to Liberation" = plus/advice.
