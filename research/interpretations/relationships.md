**Arithmetic facts (our formulas, all dates 1900–2030):**
- **D_inner can only be 3, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18 or 21** (D ≡ A+B+C and E ≡ 2(A+B+C) mod 9, so D+E ≡ 3(A+B+C)). So the relationship entry
  has only 7 possible energies; texts for the other 15 are never shown at J. (mind.university's example entry is 15 — consistent.)
- The partner point (love) and m can be any of 3–22. 1 and 2 never occur on the line (sources agree).

## Roles of the three numbers

- **mind.university:** entry (D_inner) = sits in the karmic tail, usually in minus first; partner/heart point (love) = the partner you
  attract — strong side in plus, shadow in minus — and your own task in the couple; balance (m) = whether money and love feed each other.
  Work order: karmic tail → entry into plus → balance. Work on your own minus arcanum, don't remake the partner.
- **matritsa-sudby.ru (liniya-otnoshenii):** read bottom to top: entry = principles by which a partner suits you; middle (partner) = the
  look that attracts you and how open you are; top (= our m) = blocker that keeps the first two in balance.
- **woman.ru:** pos. 1 = partner's mindset / your emotional openness; pos. 2 = partner's appearance and where you meet; pos. 3 = what pulls
  you into destructive relationships.
- **gadalkindom.ru:** M = acquaintance and readiness; R1 = general character of the couple; R = problems and their solution (formulas not given).
- **sozai (video series):** one text per arcanum for "the relationship zone", gendered (written mostly for women looking for a man).

## Summary

- Sources agree closely on partner types; the strongest single-arcanum consensus: 3 (children), 4 (protector vs. woman in male role),
  8 (official marriage), 9 (late / careful), 12 (sacrifice), 15 (dependency), 21 (foreign partner).
- Most Russian texts are written for a woman seeking a man; our texts should be gender-neutral.
- The entry point D_inner can only show 3/6/9/12/15/18/21 — our "Relationship Channel" chapter only needs those 7 texts.
- Sexuality has no agreed position; gadalkindom's version uses E, family centre and E + family centre.
- English sources number 8 and 11 the other way round; do not import their 8/11 texts.
