sources' claim that 1 and 2 never fall on the money line. (gadalkindom still prints texts for 1 and 2 at the entry point.)

## Roles of the three money-line numbers — sources disagree

- **mind.university:** entry (C_inner) = the road money comes by and what blocks it; $ (money) = sphere where work brings money and joy;
  balance (m) = how money and relationships feed or drain each other. Order: C → entry → $ → balance. Do not read $ alone.
- **matritsa-sudby.ru:** top = entry = most profitable field; middle = $ = narrows the profession; **bottom (our m) = what blocks money**.
- **numerolog.ru:** entry = main income profession; **lowest value = blocker**.
- **gadalkindom.ru:** L = entry (how you start attracting money, by action, not profession); R2 = professions; R = flow / blocking.
- **sohemvastu (EN, from zones.md):** C (year) = obstacle; C_inner = channel entrance; C_mid = past earning methods.
- Practical consequence: one text per energy is used by most Russian sites for all three slots; the role decides the emphasis
  (entry → "how money comes", $ → "which work", m → "what blocks / balance with relationships").

## Summary of agreement / disagreement

- Themes per energy are stable across 6–7 Russian sources; differences are mostly in the profession lists (MS lists ordinary jobs, MU/SZ list higher-status ones).
- **Which slot is the blocker** differs (m vs C vs lowest number) — see the roles section.
- **11 and workaholism:** NR treats hard work to exhaustion as the source of money; three others call it the block.
- **English DMC uses Rider-Waite numbering (8 Strength, 11 Justice).** Any English source must be checked before use.
- OWN (manuscript) is not zone-specific but its plus/minus for every energy agree with the money readings above; 7, 13, 19 and 22 even contain the money-related lines.
