# Детско-родительская карма (ДРК) — Эцэг эх ба хүүхдийн үйлийн үр (A, A_mid, A_inner)

Researched 2026-11-13. Notes are paraphrased themes, not quotes. The method is esoteric; schools differ.

## Positions and our chapters

| Our key | Formula | Russian | Our chapters |
|---|---|---|---|
| A (`left`) | day | А, "визитка"; in ДРК: why the soul chose these parents / what the child teaches the parents | Эцэг эхтэй харилцаа, Хүүхэдтэй харилцаа (both read A · S · O) |
| A_mid (`left.mid`, O) | A + A_inner | А2 | same |
| A_inner (`left.inner`, S) | A + E | А1 | same |

- A can be any of 1–22. **A_mid and A_inner are never 1 or 2** (checked for all dates 1900–2030; MC states the same).
  So for energies 1 and 2 only the A point is relevant in this zone.
- **Order of meaning — sources disagree:**
  - MC (matrica-sudby.ru) and MSY (matrica-sudbyy.ru): 1st = what the child teaches the parents / why the soul chose them;
    2nd = main task of the relationship; 3rd = typical mistakes. Neither gives formulas.
  - mind.university (zones.md): A_mid = task, A_inner = mistakes.
  - TC (tenchat) and Nefedova (applied.md): A = outer symptom (how the script shows now), A2 = own attitude to parenthood
    (mechanism), A1 = what came from one's own parents (root). Read from present to past.
  - Practical reading for our texts: A = what this energy teaches/brings between generations; A_mid = the task; A_inner = the
    inherited mistake/root. This fits MC + MU + TC if A1 = A_inner.
- Sources describe the lesson in the relationship, not whether/when children come (applied.md, numato).

## 1
- **+** (MC) parents learn to see their own mistakes and respect the child's feelings; (TD) child's initiative and creativity.
- **−** (MU) suppressed initiative, fear of new starts, disbelief in a rich life; (TD) child suppressed or attention-seeking.
- **Зөвлөгөө**: voice your own opinion, accept relatives as they are (MU); back the child's first steps and let them finish small projects (TD).
- **Санал нийлэх / зөрөх**: agree on "initiative". Only at A. OM's minus (self-doubt after one criticism) fits a child whose initiative was crushed.

## 2

- **Зөвлөгөө**: name feelings aloud, teach boundaries (TD); be honest with the child (MU).

## 3

## Summary
- MU, MC and TD agree on the theme of every energy; they differ only in emphasis. MC is the only one that splits task vs mistake.
- The real disagreement is the **order/meaning of the three points** (see top). Our chapters read A·S·O together, which avoids the problem.
