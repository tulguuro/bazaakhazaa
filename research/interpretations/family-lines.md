# Родовой квадрат, линии рода, родовые программы — Удмын дөрвөлжин, удмын шугам (TL, TR, BR, BL, family centre)

Researched 2026-11-13. Notes are paraphrased themes, not quotes. The method is esoteric; schools differ.

## Positions and our chapters

| Our key | Formula | Age | Russian reading | Our chapters |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TL (`tl`, F) | A + B | 10 | father's line, **spiritual** task/talent of the kin | Эцгийн үйлийн үр (Father's Karma), Эцгээс өгсөн авьяас (Father's Talents) |
| TR (`tr`, G) | B + C | 30 | mother's line, spiritual task/talent | Эхийн үйлийн үр (Mother's Karma), Эхээс өгсөн авьяас (Mother's Talents) |

| BL (`bl`, H) | D + A | 70 | mother's line, material karma | Удмын хөтөлбөр |
| familyCentre (U) | TL+TR+BR+BL | — | сила рода: combined resource, "peacemaker" of both lines | Удмын мэдээлэл (Family Comfort Zone), Удмын хөтөлбөр |

- Father's line = TL + BR, mother's line = TR + BL (MU, matrix-destiny.com, zones.md). Upper corners = talents/spiritual tasks
  (read plus first); lower corners = material/hereditary karma (read minus first). Everyone carries both lines.
- **Which numbers occur** (all dates 1900–2030, digit-sum reduction): TL is never 1 (2 only for 1 Jan / 1 Nov-type dates where
  A+B = 2); TR, BR, BL are never 1 or 2; **U is never 1, 2, 17 or 19**. So texts for 1 here are theoretical.
- **Disagreement:** a search-result summary (svitstyle/nfront, unverified, page 403) says upper corners = men's unworked karma,
  lower = women's. All readable sources say diagonals: TL–BR father, TR–BL mother. Keep ours.
- **Mismatch with our chapters:** our "Father's/Mother's Karma" chapters read only TL/TR, which Russian sources call the
  *spiritual task / talent* corners; "karma" in the Russian sense sits in the lower corners BR/BL. Consider either reading
  TL+BR / TR+BL for the karma chapters, or keep TL/TR and write them as "what the line hands you to finish".
- MU's per-arcana pages have a "родовой знак" (family sign) computed as X+X and X+(X±9) (e.g. 5+5=10, 5+14=19; 21+12, 21+3).
  This is NOT one of our positions and the formula is not explained — FLAG, not used.

## Summary
- MU/MUR and TDR describe the same themes per energy; MUR gives mostly minus + task, consistent with "corners read in minus".
- No source writes separate texts for upper vs lower corners per energy; the split is only "upper = talent/spiritual, lower =
  material karma". Our talent chapters (F, G) should use the plus; the programme chapter should use the minus and task.
- No source gives per-energy texts for the family centre; mind.university calls it a resource and peacemaker of both lines.
