# Материальная карма — Материаллаг үйлийн үр (C, C_mid, C_inner)

Researched 2026-11-13. Notes are paraphrased themes, not quotes. The method is esoteric; schools differ.

## Positions and our chapters

| Our key | Formula | Russian name | Our chapters (research/chapters.json) |
|---|---|---|---|
| C (`right`) | digit sum of year | точка В, материальная карма, "задача души в материи" | Биеийн үйлийн үр (Body Karma), Сүнсний гол зорилго (Main Soul Mission), Санхүүгийн чулуу (Financial Stone) |
| C_inner (`right.inner`) | C + E | вход в денежный канал (c1) | Санхүүгийн эхлэл (Financial Start) |
| C_mid (`right.mid`) | C + C_inner | middle point of the material triad | Санхүүгийн эрчим (Financial Energy) |

- Which numbers can occur (all dates 1900–2030, digit-sum reduction): C is never 1, and C = 2 only for the year 2000.
  C_inner and C_mid are never 1 or 2. Two sources (MS, NR) say the same: 1 and 2 never fall on the money line.
- **C_mid has no per-energy text in any source found.** Generic meaning only: skills picked up fast, a past-life way of earning;
  income from it covers basic needs (mind.university denezhnyy-kanal); MS calls the middle number the one that "narrows the career
  choice". Write C_mid texts from the energy's general meaning + "fast skill / old way of earning".
- What C is: material karma, money scenario carried from past lives, attitude to material goods; money suffers first, then health
  (MU, zones.md). vikna.tv: material standing and financial base. Lisovskaya (free excerpt): attitude to finance + a karmic task.
  English sohemvastu reads the far-right number C as the "obstacle to abundance" (minority view).

## 1
- **+** C: (never occurs at C/C_in in modern dates). MU year: courage to start, self-belief, acting for others' good.
- **−** C: pride/egoism OR disbelief in self, fear of starting, dropping things half-done.
- **Зөвлөгөө / совет**: believe in yourself, find work you love, learn to forgive. MU entry: money follows thought turned into action; share ideas to find partners. Prof (MU): leader, scientist, inventor, jeweller/tailor, coach, teacher, writer, journalist.
- **Санал нийлэх / зөрөх**: OM's two minuses (arrogance vs self-doubt) match MU exactly. Only MU covers it; it is theoretical for this zone.

## Summary of agreement
- Sources agree strongly on C_inner (money entry) meanings and professions; MS/NR/TD are near-copies of one tradition.
- Point C itself is thinly described (MU only, often minus only). No source describes C_mid per energy.
- Our chapters read C three ways (body, soul mission, financial stone). Russian sources support "material karma / money + health"
  (body and money); "soul mission" at C is supported only by MU's wording "задача души в материи" for 17 and 22. Other sources put
  "задача души" at D (see soul-challenge.md).
